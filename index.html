How this works

Marriage uses 3 decks, 21 cards each. The first player to show three pure melds turns the tiplu (main joker); only seen players know it. Before you show, you can only use public actions by players who have already seen it. A seen player never drops the tiplu, alter, or the 2 ordinary jokers, so Dropped / Not picked hard-eliminates every tiplu of that rank, in all suits. The same card also halves the two same-suit neighbours (the tiplus for which it would be poplu/jhiplu), because a seer usually protects those but not always. Picked is weak evidence: each pick multiplies that tiplu's score by 2.5, so a picked 3♥ boosts 2♥ and 4♥; tapping Picked again stacks. A pick that is later dropped/passed is rejected and adds nothing. Scores normalize to 100%; with no input every tiplu sits at 1/52 ≈ 1.9%. If the top tiplus are tied, the app lists the best cards to watch next. Picked then Dropped locks.
