How this works

Marriage uses 3 decks, 21 cards each. The first player to show three pure melds turns the tiplu (main joker); only seen players know it. A seen player never drops the tiplu, poplu, jhiplu, alter (or ordinary jokers, if protected), so a Dropped card is not maal. If a seen player leaves a discard in the pile (Not picked), it is also not maal — they would pick a valuable card. Picked alone proves nothing: it could be for a sequence. Picks are soft evidence: each picked card that is wild for a tiplu multiplies that tiplu's score, and scores are normalized to 100%. With no input every card sits at 1/52 ≈ 1.9%. A card marked Picked and then Dropped is locked and cannot be picked again. If none remain, a seen player touched a protected card — a misinput or a leak.
